Cast Iron Chef Butcher Selections

All of our meat is aged and cut on premises. The steaks will be cooked slightly under your desired temperature as they will continue to cook in your sizzling hot cast iron pan. Should it need to be cooked more, we will bring out a hot cast iron pan and cook your steak table side. Due to the nature of cooking in cast iron, all of our steaks will have an even char on the meat. We highly discourage any of the steaks below from being cooked above medium well to ensure the utmost quality and flavor of the steak. All of our steaks and chops are gluten-free. Sub garlic mashed potatoes to any steak +$7.
